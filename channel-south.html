A full feasibility-stage metocean characterisation conducted independently, using open datasets and a reproducible Python pipeline. Study site

The study area is located in the central English Channel, approximately 20 km south of the Isle of Wight. This area is of direct relevance to offshore wind development, lying within the broader Rampion wind farm cluster and adjacent to the proposed extension zones in the English Channel. The region presents a technically challenging metocean environment: a mixed swell and wind-sea wave climate dominated by Atlantic swell from the southwest, complex tidal dynamics including the well-documented Solent double high water, and shallow-to-intermediate water depths (30–80 m).

Analysis modules

🌬
Wind Climate
25-year ERA5 10 m wind climatology. Weibull distribution fit for speed statistics. Directional analysis with wind rose. Seasonal decomposition. Persistent high-wind event characterisation for operational planning.
→ ERA5 · scipy.stats · windrose
🌊
Wave Climate
CMEMS global wave reanalysis (WAM). Hs–Tp joint distribution. Swell / wind-sea spectral decomposition. Model validation against CEFAS WaveNet buoy observations (r = 0.91, RMSE = 0.39 m). Wave rose and directional statistics.
→ CMEMS · WaveNet · xarray · scipy
🌙
Tidal Analysis
BODC Portsmouth tide gauge harmonic analysis using UTide. Constituent table (M2, S2, N2, K1, O1 and higher). Spring-neap characterisation. Characterisation of the Solent double high water, a hydrodynamic feature of the region driven by co-oscillation and bathymetric effects.
→ BODC · UTide · FES2014
📈
Extreme Value Analysis
Peaks-over-threshold (POT) method using pyextremes. Generalised Pareto distribution (GPD) fit. 50- and 100-year return period estimates for Hs and wind speed with confidence intervals. Storm season analysis for operational scheduling.
→ pyextremes · GPD · POT

Tools & pipeline

All analysis was conducted in Python with a fully reproducible pipeline version-controlled on GitHub. Key libraries: xarray, pandas, scipy, matplotlib, cartopy, UTide, pyextremes, windrose, geopandas. Data accessed programmatically via the Copernicus CDS API and CMEMS toolbox.

The report is structured to industry feasibility-study standards, covering site description, data sources and methodology, wind climate, wave climate, tidal analysis, extreme value analysis, and a marine operations weather window summary.
