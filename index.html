About Me

I'm a physical oceanographer and numerical modeller with a background spanning academic research and coastal engineering. My doctoral work at LOPS (Université de Bretagne Occidentale / Ifremer, Brest) focused on hydrothermal plume dynamics at the Lucky Strike vent field, using CROCO in hydrostatic, non-hydrostatic (NHMG), and non-Boussinesq (NBQ) configurations on national HPC infrastructure.

I work across the full modelling pipeline — from domain configuration and source-code modification in Fortran to post-processing and visualisation in Python. I'm comfortable reading and modifying model internals, compiling on HPC clusters, and diagnosing numerical behaviour at the scheme level.

More recently I've been building metocean analysis skills relevant to offshore renewable energy, including wind and wave climate characterisation, tidal harmonic analysis, and extreme value analysis using open datasets and reproducible Python workflows.

I hold two Masters degrees: Applied Mechanical Sciences / Hydrodynamics and Ocean Engineering (École Centrale de Nantes) and Ocean, Atmosphere, Climate & Spatial Observation (Sorbonne Université).

Doctoral Research
Hydrothermal Plume Dynamics at Lucky Strike
LOPS · UBO · 2018–2023 · Mid-Atlantic Ridge, 37°N

High-resolution numerical study of a hydrothermal plume in a rotating, stratified ocean. Ran CROCO in three dynamical configurations — hydrostatic, non-hydrostatic multigrid (NHMG), and non-Boussinesq (NBQ) — on national HPC infrastructure. Key results include the first 3D rotating confirmation of the Speer & Rona (1989) cold-core anticyclone mechanism and a crossflow regime transition characterised by a source Rossby number scaling.

Channel South: Metocean Site Characterisation
Independent Study · 2026 · South of Isle of Wight, English Channel

Feasibility-stage metocean characterisation for an offshore wind development site south of the Isle of Wight. Covers 24-year wind climate, wave climate with model validation against WaveNet buoy data (r = 0.91), tidal harmonic analysis of the complex Solent double-high-water regime, and extreme value analysis (50- and 100-year return periods). Fully reproducible Python pipeline on GitHub.
